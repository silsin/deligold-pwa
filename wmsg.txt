style(wallet): give WalletView a real design

WalletView was the only view with no <style> block at all, so it rendered as
raw unstyled text: the balance hero, holdings, ledger rows and the action
buttons had no layout, spacing, colour or states.

Adds a scoped stylesheet matching the app tokens (--accent, --surface,
--card, --up/--down, --radius-*) and the existing sheet/portfolio language:

- balance hero with value, equity/gold-value/PnL cells and 3 actions
- holdings rows with icon, avg cost, weight and live PnL tone
- ledger rows with per-kind icon/amount tones
- empty states, alerts, refresh spinner, <=360px adjustments

Also fixes the corruption from the incremental append that produced this
file: CSS that had landed after </style> (so .holding__swap, .alert and the
wallet-spin keyframes were silently dropped) and two unclosed blocks
(.act .material-icons, .holding__side small) that failed the build.

Verified: build passes, all 65 template classes resolve to a rule, and the
selectors are present in the emitted CSS bundle.
